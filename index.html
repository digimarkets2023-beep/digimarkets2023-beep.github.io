Sarru Apps

Simple, ad-supported mobile apps — Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the following apps published by the developer (collectively, "the apps"):

  • Chess Master
  • 2048
  • Snake
  • Tic-Tac-Toe
  • Memory Match

Summary

These apps do not require an account and do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. They are designed to work offline, and all your game settings and scores are kept only on your own device.

What these apps do not do

  • They do not ask you to sign up or log in.
  • They do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or location.
  • They do not use a server to store your games, scores, or settings — everything stays on your device.

Advertising

These apps show ads through Google AdMob to keep them free. AdMob may use an advertising identifier and other technical information (such as general device and app information) to show and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own policies. We do not receive or store any of this information ourselves. You can learn more about how Google uses this information, and manage your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Online play (Chess Master only)

Chess Master's optional "Host a Game" / "Join a Game" feature connects two devices directly using a temporary, randomly generated room code, so two people can play together. The connection is peer-to-peer: moves travel directly between the two players' devices. No game data, chat, or personal information is stored on any server operated by us. It uses standard, publicly available internet infrastructure (a connection-broker and relay servers) only to help two devices find each other; no account or personal information is required.

Children's privacy

These apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
